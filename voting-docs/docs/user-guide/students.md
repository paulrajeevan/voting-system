# Student Guide

<span class="badge--student">Student</span>

As a student, you have three core abilities in Votable:

1. **Upload** your portfolio website
2. **Browse** all submitted portfolios
3. **Vote** for your top 3 favourites with ranked points

---

## Step 1 — Log In

See [Getting Started → Logging In](./getting-started#logging-in) for full login instructions.

- If you have a `@dreamspace.academy` email, use **Sign in with Google**.
- If an admin provisioned your account, use the **Username & Password** form.

On your first Google login, select **Student** on the Role Selection Screen.

---

## Step 2 — Upload Your Portfolio

Once you land on the **Student Dashboard**, locate the **Portfolio Upload** section.

:::tip You can only upload once
Each student account can have **one portfolio entry**. You can edit it later, but you cannot create a second one.
:::

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Description | Rules |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Title** | Name of your project | Required |
| **URL** | Link to your portfolio site | Must start with `https://` |
| **Description** | Short summary of your project | Required |

After submitting, your portfolio will appear in the **Portfolio Gallery** visible to all logged-in users.

### Editing Your Portfolio

To update your submission, return to the Portfolio Upload section. Your existing data will be pre-filled. Make your changes and click **Save**.

---

## Step 3 — Browse the Portfolio Gallery

The **Portfolio Gallery** shows all submitted portfolios in a responsive card grid.

- **Search / Filter:** Use the search bar to find portfolios by name or project title.
- **Hover Effects:** Cards lift on hover with an animated shadow — click any card to visit the portfolio URL.

---

## Step 4 — Cast Your Votes

:::info Voting is a one-time action
Once you submit your ballot, your choices are recorded. Revisit the voting section to see your selections.
:::

### How the Ranking Works

| Rank | Points Awarded |
|------|---------------|
| 🥇 1st Choice | **5 points** |
| 🥈 2nd Choice | **3 points** |
| 🥉 3rd Choice | **2 points** |

### Casting Your Ballot — Step by Step

1. Browse the **Portfolio Gallery**.
2. On any portfolio card, click **"Rank 1st"**, **"Rank 2nd"**, or **"Rank 3rd"** to assign that portfolio to a ballot slot.
3. Check the **My Ballot Drawer** — the sticky panel at the bottom of the screen — to review your current selections:
   - 1st Choice (5 pts)
   - 2nd Choice (3 pts)
   - 3rd Choice (2 pts)
4. You can change your choices at any time **before** clicking **"Submit Votes"**.
5. When satisfied, click **Submit Votes** to record your ballot.

### Voting Rules

- You **must** assign exactly 3 different portfolios (one per rank).
- You **cannot** rank the same portfolio more than once.
- You **can** vote for your own portfolio.
- You **cannot** change your vote after submitting.

---

## Viewing the Scoreboard

The scoreboard is only visible **after an admin releases the results**. When it appears:

- Portfolios are ordered **highest points to lowest**.
- Each entry shows **Portfolio Title** and **Total Points** only.
- No voter names, ballot choices, or individual voting records are ever displayed.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can staff or admins see who I voted for?**
No. Individual ballot data is protected by database-level Row Level Security. Only you can see your own votes.

**What if I don't have a `@dreamspace.academy` email?**
Ask your Administrator to provision credentials for you. You'll receive a username and password to log in.

**Can I update my portfolio after voting?**
Yes — portfolio submissions and voting are separate. You can edit your portfolio at any time (while voting is open).

**What happens if results are not yet released?**
The scoreboard will be hidden. You can still browse portfolios and manage your own ballot.
